Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dominion Labs Inc. (“Dominion Labs,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information in connection with our websites (including dmnlabs.org), our mobile applications (including Perfect Circle: One Tap and our other games), and our related products and services (collectively, the “Services”). We are the controller of the personal information processed through the Services.

By using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Services.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide to Us

  • Account and authentication information, such as your name and email address, including when you sign in using a third-party provider such as Google or Apple;
  • Transaction and billing information when you make a purchase; payments are processed by our payment processors (such as Square on the web, and Apple for in-app purchases), which handle your payment card details — we receive confirmation and limited transaction information, not your full card number;
  • Communications and support requests, including the contents of messages and feedback you send us; and
  • Any other information you choose to provide.

Information We Collect Automatically

  • Device and technical information, such as device or browser type, operating system, language and region, and app or site version;
  • Log and usage information, such as pages and screens viewed, features used, referring pages, and interactions;
  • Cookies and similar technologies on our websites (see “Cookies” below), and identifiers used for analytics and fraud prevention;
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address (we do not collect precise GPS location);
  • In our mobile games, advertising identifiers (such as the IDFA, where you grant permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt) and ad-interaction data, collected by us and our advertising partners; and
  • Diagnostic and performance information used to keep the Services stable and secure.

Information From Third Parties

We receive information from authentication providers (such as Google and Apple), payment processors (such as Square and Apple), and analytics and advertising partners, consistent with their policies and your settings.

2. How We Use Personal Information

  • Provide, operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Services;
  • Create and manage accounts and authenticate users;
  • Process and validate transactions and purchases;
  • Measure and understand how the Services are used through analytics;
  • In our games, serve, mediate, measure, and — where permitted — personalize advertising;
  • Communicate with you, including responding to inquiries and sending service-related messages;
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents; and
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our websites use cookies and similar technologies for essential functionality, security, and analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling some cookies may affect functionality. Where required, we obtain consent for non-essential cookies.

4. Advertising and Analytics

We use analytics providers (such as Cloudflare and, in our apps, Google Firebase/Google Analytics) to understand usage. Our games are supported by advertising and use AppLovin MAX to mediate ads across networks that may include AppLovin, Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, Mintegral, Liftoff/Vungle, DT Exchange, InMobi, Chartboost, and Ogury. On Apple devices we request permission through App Tracking Transparency before using the IDFA for cross-app tracking; if you decline, we and our partners will not use the IDFA to track you across apps and websites, and you may receive less relevant (contextual) advertising. You can control ad personalization through your device settings and our partners’ opt-out mechanisms.

5. How We Disclose Personal Information

We do not sell personal information for money. We disclose personal information:

  • To service providers and processors who perform services on our behalf (such as hosting and content delivery, payment processing, authentication, analytics, and support), under contracts that limit their use of the information;
  • To advertising partners in connection with our games, who receive identifiers and ad-interaction data to serve and measure advertising; depending on your jurisdiction, some of this activity may be considered “sharing” or a “sale” for cross-context behavioral advertising (see the region-specific sections below);
  • To comply with law, enforce our terms, or protect the rights, safety, and security of our users, the public, or Dominion Labs;
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets; and
  • With your consent or at your direction.

6. Your Choices

  • Account: review and update your information, or request deletion, by contacting us;
  • Communications: opt out of non-essential emails via the unsubscribe link or by contacting us;
  • Cookies: control cookies through your browser;
  • Advertising (games): use App Tracking Transparency and your device’s advertising settings; and
  • Access and deletion: contact us to exercise the rights described below.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including to provide the Services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and enforce our agreements, after which we delete or anonymize it.

8. Security

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, use, alteration, and destruction, appropriate to its sensitivity. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children’s Privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us at [email protected] and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

10. International Data Transfers

Dominion Labs is based in the United States, and we and our service providers may process personal information in the United States and other countries whose data-protection laws may differ from those in your country. Where required, we implement appropriate safeguards for such transfers, as described in the region-specific sections below.

11. Third-Party Links and Services

The Services integrate and link to third-party services (such as authentication, payments, advertising, analytics, and app stores). This Privacy Policy does not apply to those third parties, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice. Your continued use of the Services after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.

13. Contact Us

For questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, contact us at [email protected].
